# FocusLock 1.0.19

## Account and app discovery

- Frequently selected apps are remembered locally and surfaced earlier in the app list.
- Replaced the compact account list with a clearer Account section.
- Added account details, subscription status, FAQ, Privacy Policy, Terms, Contact us, password/email changes, sign out, and account deletion.
- Removed the diagnostics toggle from the visible Account section so it stays focused on user controls.
- Google sign-in behavior is unchanged; its displayed OAuth project name is controlled by Google Auth Platform branding and Supabase domain settings.
